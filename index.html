About Georgia Slopes

Independent hobby project

Slope angle

Computed in your browser from SRTM elevation data (about 30 m resolution) using Horn's method. Short steep steps, cliffs and gullies narrower than about 60 m are smoothed out, so real slopes can be steeper than shown.

Tour planner

Time estimate: 4 km/h on the flat plus 400 m of ascent per hour; the larger value plus half the smaller. Descent is not counted. The profile is coloured by the terrain slope beside the track.
